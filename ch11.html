1. What fundamental distinction separates clustering from classification in machine learning workflows?
  • A) Classification handles continuous targets, whereas clustering handles discrete ordinal variables.

19. Given points $P_1 = (2, 10)$ and $P_2 = (5, 8)$, what is the Euclidean distance $d(P_1, P_2)$ between them?

36. What is the mathematical definition of the Silhouette Coefficient $s(i)$ for an instance $i$?
  • A) $s(i) = \frac{a(i) - b(i)}{\min(a(i), b(i))}$
  • B) $s(i) = \frac{b(i) - a(i)}{\max(a(i), b(i))}$
  • C) $s(i) = a(i) \times b(i)$
  • D) $s(i) = \frac{a(i) + b(i)}{2}$
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Silhouette score $s(i) = \frac{b(i) - a(i)}{\max(a(i), b(i))}$, where $a(i)$ is mean intra-cluster distance and $b(i)$ is mean nearest-cluster distance.

  • C) $a(i)$: total points in cluster; $b(i)$: total clusters.
  • D) $a(i)$: variance of feature 1; $b(i)$: variance of feature 2.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: $a(i)$ measures internal cluster compactness (cohesion), while $b(i)$ measures distance to the closest adjacent cluster (separation).
